# Bundled sound effects

These five files are selected from Kenney's **UI Audio** and **Impact Sounds** packs:

- `kenney-click1.ogg`
- `kenney-switch1.ogg`
- `kenney-impact-light.ogg`
- `kenney-impact-soft.ogg`
- `kenney-bell.ogg`

Kenney publishes both source packs under **Creative Commons CC0 1.0**. They may be used, modified and distributed, including commercially, without attribution.

- UI Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
- Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source archives were downloaded from the official Kenney asset pages on 2026-08-10. The files are kept under their original Ogg/Vorbis encoding; only filenames were normalized for the platform catalog.
